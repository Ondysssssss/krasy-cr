Krásy ČR

A private desktop tool for creating and scheduling short videos about Czech landmarks and heritage sites.

What it is

Krásy ČR runs locally on its author's computer. It assembles short vertical videos about castles, ruins, lookout towers and other places worth visiting in the Czech Republic, and schedules them to the author's own social media accounts.

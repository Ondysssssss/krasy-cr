Privacy Policy

Krásy ČR · Last updated: 4 September 2026

1. Scope

Krásy ČR is a desktop application that runs locally on its author's computer and is used to manage his own social media accounts. It has no server component and no user accounts.

2. What data is handled

The application does not collect, store or transmit any personal data of third parties.

Access tokens for connected accounts (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok) are stored exclusively on the local computer and are never sent anywhere else.

3. How the data is used

The application communicates only with the official APIs of the connected platforms, for the sole purpose of uploading videos to accounts the user has connected themselves. No data is used for any other purpose, sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

4. Analytics and tracking

The application contains no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

5. Deleting your data

Disconnecting an account in the application removes its stored token from the local computer. Access can also be revoked at any time in the settings of the relevant platform.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date of the most recent change is shown above.
